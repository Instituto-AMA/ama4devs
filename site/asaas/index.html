Asaas, with a clock you move.

The Asaas API v3 on localhost, with the business rules captured from the real sandbox. Skip days and watch billing happen: subscriptions charge the saved card on the due date and retry when it is refused, boletos go overdue with their fine and interest, cards credit at D+32, and every webhook reaches your machine. Debug your payment state machine end to end, not by guessing.

The fastest way: the ready image, straight from GHCR, no login.

docker pull ghcr.io/instituto-ama/asaas-mock:latest
docker run -d -p 45445:45445 -e CLOCK_MODE=VIRTUAL_FLOWING \
  ghcr.io/instituto-ama/asaas-mock:latest
Panel and API at localhost:45445, with the virtual clock on

To run from the source, or after changing the mock.

git clone https://github.com/Instituto-AMA/ama4devs.git
cd ama4devs
docker build -t asaas-mock mocks/asaas
docker run -d -p 45445:45445 -e CLOCK_MODE=VIRTUAL_FLOWING asaas-mock
Panel and API at localhost:45445

A real run of the image: a saved card set to approve three charges pays three invoices on their due dates, then the fourth is refused at 08:00, 14:00 and 20:00 and three more times the next day, as Asaas retries, and goes overdue. Only the paper is drawn.

The clock is the difference.

On the real sandbox, time is real: a card payment credits in 32 days and an overdue boleto takes a day to show up. Here every simulated day runs the jobs Asaas runs, in order, so you see your system react to months of billing in seconds.

Each day, a tick

  • Subscriptions create their next charges, 40 days ahead.
  • A saved card is charged on the due date at 08:00, 14:00 and 20:00, and retried the next day.
  • Unpaid charges go OVERDUE; the fine once, interest per day.
  • Boleto credits at D+1 business day, card at D+32, debit at D+3.
  • Webhooks fire and retry on Asaas's own schedule.
  • Payer reminders and notices go out by e-mail and WhatsApp.
  • API keys with an expiry date expire.
curl -X POST localhost:45445/_admin/clock/advance \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"days": 32}'

Three modes

  • REAL: the machine's time, like the sandbox; the clock refuses to skip.
  • VIRTUAL_FLOWING: time flows, and you skip ahead when you want. The default in the repository's compose.
  • VIRTUAL_FROZEN: the clock moves only when a test says so. Made for CI: the same run gives the same result.

Use cases.

Scenarios your payment code has to survive, each one a few calls away. They assume the quick start's container: key $aact_hmlg_local, clock in VIRTUAL_FLOWING.

A saved card that pays three months and fails the fourth

Proves your subscription state machine end to end, instead of by guessing: invoices paid by the saved card on their due dates, then a refused renewal. Asaas tries the card at 08:00, 14:00 and 20:00 on the due date and three more times the next day; each refusal is a PAYMENT_CREDIT_CARD_CAPTURE_REFUSED while the charge stays PENDING, then PAYMENT_OVERDUE and no more attempts. Does your app warn the customer on the first refusal, keep access during the retries, and suspend only when the charge goes overdue?

# a saved card that approves three charges, then declines
curl -X PUT localhost:45445/_admin/credit-cards/<creditCardToken> \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"approvalsLeft":3}'
curl localhost:45445/v3/subscriptions -H 'access_token: $aact_hmlg_local' \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"customer":"cus_…","billingType":"CREDIT_CARD",
  "value":129.9,"nextDueDate":"2026-11-05","cycle":"MONTHLY","creditCardToken":"<creditCardToken>",
  "creditCardHolderInfo":{…},"remoteIp":"127.0.0.1"}'
curl -X POST localhost:45445/_admin/clock/advance -d '{"days":102}' -H 'content-type: application/json'
# from 27/10 to 06/02, the day after the fourth due date:
# → 3 × PAYMENT_CONFIRMED, then 6 × PAYMENT_CREDIT_CARD_CAPTURE_REFUSED and PAYMENT_OVERDUE

Swap the card with PUT /v3/subscriptions/{id}/creditCard during the retries and the next attempt captures with the new one. The schedule is configuration: CARD_CAPTURE_SCHEDULE or the panel's test cards page.

A card charge that credits 32 days later

Proves your order flow tells PAYMENT_CONFIRMED (the customer paid) from PAYMENT_RECEIVED (the money is yours), and that your balance view waits for the second.

curl localhost:45445/v3/payments -H 'access_token: $aact_hmlg_local' \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"customer":"cus_…","billingType":"CREDIT_CARD",
  "value":350,"dueDate":"2026-10-27","creditCardToken":"…","remoteIp":"127.0.0.1"}'
# → CONFIRMED, netValue 339.04, estimatedCreditDate 2026-11-30
curl -X POST localhost:45445/_admin/clock/advance -d '{"days":34}' -H 'content-type: application/json'
# → webhook PAYMENT_RECEIVED; balance 339.04

A boleto paid late, with fine and interest

Proves you charge what Asaas charges: the 2% fine once on the first overdue day, the 1% a month interest pro rata over a 30-day commercial month. R$ 120,00 paid six days late is R$ 122,64.

curl localhost:45445/v3/payments -H 'access_token: $aact_hmlg_local' \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"customer":"cus_…","billingType":"BOLETO",
  "value":120,"dueDate":"2026-12-02","fine":{"value":2},"interest":{"value":1}}'
curl -X POST localhost:45445/_admin/clock/advance -d '{"days":6}' -H 'content-type: application/json'
# → webhook PAYMENT_OVERDUE on 03/12
curl -X POST localhost:45445/v3/sandbox/payment/pay_…/confirm -H 'access_token: $aact_hmlg_local'
# → CONFIRMED, value 122.64; one day later PAYMENT_RECEIVED

Your webhook answers 204

Proves your endpoint answers what Asaas accepts. Only HTTP 200 counts: 201 and 204 are failures, the real rule. A failed delivery retries 15 times on Asaas's table (now, +30 s, +1 min, +3 min 30 s, +5 min, +15 min, +25 min, +1 h five times, +2 h twice, +3 h). With SEQUENTIALLY, the event behind it waits; after the 15th the webhook is interrupted and the whole queue stops.

curl localhost:45445/_admin/webhooks/deliveries   # every attempt, with your endpoint's status and body

A saved card that stops working

On the real sandbox a card that declines never becomes a token, so a saved card always approves. Here the test card 4000000000000341 tokenizes and then declines every charge made with its token, with the real error (invalid_action, same sentence): the renewal that fails months later, before it happens in production.

curl localhost:45445/v3/payments -H 'access_token: $aact_hmlg_local' \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"customer":"cus_…","billingType":"CREDIT_CARD",
  "value":99.9,"dueDate":"2026-11-10","creditCardToken":"<token of 4000000000000341>"}'
# → 400 {"errors":[{"code":"invalid_action","description":"Transação não autorizada. …"}]}

Installments that add up

Proves your split of a total matches Asaas's: the rounding remainder goes to the last installment, the card fee truncates (R$ 350 in 12x is R$ 1,20 of fee per installment, not R$ 1,21), and on a card installment n credits at D+32×n.

curl localhost:45445/v3/payments -H 'access_token: $aact_hmlg_local' \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"customer":"cus_…","billingType":"BOLETO",
  "totalValue":350,"installmentCount":12,"dueDate":"2026-11-10"}'

What the payer receives

Every charge event tells the payer by e-mail (in Mailpit) and by a WhatsApp template (in the WhatsApp mock). An installment plan is told once. Run the repository's compose and open the three screens side by side.
